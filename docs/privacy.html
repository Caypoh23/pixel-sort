Pixel Sort privacy note

Pixel Sort is an early prototype of a puzzle game. This page explains what the web version collects.

What we collect

  • Anonymous gameplay events: which level you play, when you finish it, how many moves it took, and whether you used undo or an extra tube.
  • Your answer if you reply to the question about installing the full game.
  • Technical data your browser sends with every request: device type, browser, approximate country, the page that sent you here and campaign tags in the link.

We do not ask for your name, email or phone number, and the game has no accounts.

Tools we use

  • PostHog stores the gameplay events so we can see where players get stuck. It keeps a random ID in your browser storage.
  • Meta Pixel may be used to measure our ads on Facebook and Instagram. It can set a cookie and is covered by Meta's own privacy policy.

Your game progress (finished pictures, sound setting) is saved only in your own browser.

Your choices

You can block cookies and tracking in your browser settings or with a content blocker; the game keeps working. Clearing your browser data removes the saved progress and the random ID.
